# Sources — every story on the page

Checked 23–24 November 2026 against reporting of 21–22 November 2026.
Where a figure moved during the day, the page uses the figure reported
on 22 November itself.

## Lead

1. **COP31: no deal at longest summit (Antalya, Turkey).** The summit
   ended on Sunday after two weeks of talks and two days of overtime; the
   EU and about 130 parties rejected a draft that made no mention of
   fossil fuels. The longest UN climate summit on record, and the first
   to end with no deal since Copenhagen in 2009. Sources: BBC, Reuters,
   CNN, Le Monde, AP (22 Nov).

## Grid stories

1. **Ukraine sank Russian warship (Benghazi, Libya).** Kyiv says its sea
   drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi port on Sunday;
   a second vessel was also hit. Seven sea drones were used, and the
   cargo ship MV Angara and a Ka-27 helicopter were also hit. Sources:
   AP, One Turn, Al Jazeera, Euronews (22–23 Nov; the attack itself was
   Sunday 22 Nov).
2. **Flats collapse in strike near Kyiv (Dymer, Ukraine).** A Russian
   drone partly collapsed a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north
   of Kyiv; 4 killed as reported on Sunday evening, a boy pulled alive
   from the rubble. (AP reported at least 3 earlier on Sunday; the toll
   rose to 11 by Monday morning as bodies were recovered.) Sources: AP,
   Zelenskyy on X, Ukrinform (22 Nov).
3. **Supermarket hit in Vyshneve (Kyiv region, Ukraine).** A Russian
   drone struck a crowded grocery store in Vyshneve, south-west of Kyiv;
   3 killed and 15 hurt, including 3 children. Sources: Zelenskyy on X,
   Vyshneve mayor, Kyiv regional officials, Zmina, EU Today (22 Nov).
4. **Three Russian missiles crossed into Poland.** Polish prosecutors
   confirmed on Sunday that all three objects that crashed in south-east
   Poland on Friday were Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles. NATO's North
   Atlantic Council meets on Monday. Sources: Reuters (22 Nov), AP.
5. **Democrats win House by 258 (USA).** Democrat Chuck Hubbard beat
   Republican Virginia Foxx by 330 votes in North Carolina's 5th District
   after a recount; Democrats hold at least 258 House seats, their
   biggest majority since 1992. Sources: BBC, AP (22 Nov).
6. **Alcaraz wins first ATP Finals title (Turin, Italy).** Carlos Alcaraz
   beat world No. 1 Alexander Zverev 6-2, 7-6(7) in the final. Sources:
   Reuters, ATP (22 Nov).
7. **Iran strikes hit Bahrain site.** Iran struck a civilian industrial
   facility in Sitra, Bahrain; two people hurt. Tankers were also hit
   near the Strait of Hormuz. Sources: Reuters, AFP, Al Jazeera
   (21–22 Nov).
8. **El Chapo Isidro captured (Guasave, Mexico).** Troops seized FBI
   most-wanted cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in a Friday-night
   raid; 11 killed in the fighting — seven suspected gunmen and four
   soldiers. Sources: AP, CNN (21 Nov).
